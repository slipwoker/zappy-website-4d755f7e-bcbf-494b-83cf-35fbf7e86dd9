The Problem

In many cases, epoxy is the best grout. The problem starts with the application.

Keeps water out

Epoxy grout is completely waterproof. It doesn't absorb, doesn't yellow, and doesn't grow mold — not in the shower, not in the pool, and not in a hard-working kitchen.

Built to last for years

Hard as stone, doesn't crumble, doesn't crack. Fill it once, and forget about it.

And only one thing stops it - The Application

The material hardens quickly and spreads over every tile, and anyone who doesn't clean it up in time is left with the stain. That's why most professionals simply give up on it.

The PAVEL machine presents a revolutionary approach

  • The material goes into the joint, not smeared across the tile.
  • Constant pressure fills the joint all the way to the bottom, evenly.
  • Work at a walking pace, no race against the clock.
  • At the end of the day there's nothing to wash: whatever touched the epoxy gets thrown away.

Compatible with any two-component epoxy grout gun already available on the market.

1

Mix

Both components go into a kneading bag and are mixed inside it. No bucket, no mixer.

2

Fill

Press the material into a disposable sleeve bag, close the cap, and the system is ready.

3

Run Along the Grout Lines

Guide the filling nozzle along the joint. The material goes in, the tile stays clean.

4

Toss and move on

Out of material? Bag in the bin, load a new one. At the end of the day, toss the tube and the filling nozzle too.
